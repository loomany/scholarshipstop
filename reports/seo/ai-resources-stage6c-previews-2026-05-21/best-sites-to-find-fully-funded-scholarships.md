# Best Sites to Find Fully Funded Scholarships

Finding real full funding is harder than finding “scholarship lists.” Many pages mix tuition discounts, partial awards, expired calls, and programs that only apply to local students. The best strategy is to use fully funded scholarship websites for discovery, then confirm every requirement on the official provider page.

## Quick Answer

The strongest search setup combines three sources: a modern discovery platform, official government scholarship portals, and university funding pages. ScholarshipTop is useful for filtering opportunities by country, category, provider, and profile, while official portals remain the final source for deadlines and rules.

Start at /scholarships for broad browsing, use /scholarships/hub/matches for profile-based research, and keep provider pages open for verification.

## Best Sites and Portals to Check First

No single site captures every fully funded award. Use the cards below as a practical stack rather than a one-site solution.

### ScholarshipTop

Best for: International students who want clean scholarship discovery by destination, field, funding type, and profile.

Strengths: Easier browsing than many older scholarship search websites, useful category pages such as /scholarships/category/stem and /scholarships/category/education, plus resources at /resources for application planning.

Watch out for: It is newer than some legacy U.S.-focused directories, so you should still verify details on provider pages.

Signup: Browse first; use profile tools when you want more focused matches.

International usefulness: High, especially for comparing options across countries.

### Government scholarship portals

Best for: Flagship national programs, exchange awards, and public funding.

Strengths: Official eligibility rules, trusted deadlines, and direct application instructions. For example, the U.S. Department of State explains the <a href="https://exchanges.state.gov/non-us/program/fulbright-foreign-student-program" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Fulbright Foreign Student Program</a> for non-U.S. applicants.

Watch out for: Sites can be formal, country-specific, and less searchable than scholarship discovery platforms.

Signup: Usually only required when an application cycle opens.

International usefulness: Very high for fully funded scholarships for international students.

### University scholarship pages

Best for: Graduate assistantships, doctoral funding, merit awards, and department-level packages.

Strengths: They reveal funding tied to admission, research groups, and academic departments. University pages are essential when funding depends on being accepted into a specific program.

Watch out for: Information may be spread across admissions, financial aid, and department pages.

Signup: Usually no account until you apply for admission.

International usefulness: High, but eligibility varies sharply by program.

### International organization portals

Best for: Development, public policy, education, health, and global leadership funding.

Strengths: Organizations such as the World Bank list official opportunities like <a href="https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/scholarships" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">World Bank scholarship programs</a>, and UNESCO shares education-related fellowships through <a href="https://www.unesco.org/en/fellowships" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">UNESCO fellowship information</a>.

Watch out for: Many awards target specific countries, career stages, or development priorities.

Signup: Depends on the provider.

International usefulness: Strong for applicants with a public service or development profile.

## How We Evaluated These Options

A good scholarship site should help you narrow choices without hiding the official source. We prioritized accuracy, filtering, international relevance, provider transparency, and whether the site helps students avoid wasting time on partial awards labeled as full funding.

Use this quick checklist when judging any international scholarship database:

- Does it show funding type clearly: tuition, stipend, travel, insurance, or all costs?
- Does it link or point to the official provider?
- Are deadlines current or clearly marked by cycle?
- Can you filter by degree level, country, and field?
- Does it avoid forcing paid access before showing basic scholarship details?

## A Practical Workflow for Finding Full Funding

1. Define your profile first. Write down citizenship, target degree, field, GPA range, work experience, language scores, and preferred countries.

2. Search broadly on discovery tools. Use ScholarshipTop, /scholarships, and /scholarships/hub/matches to build a shortlist instead of opening random search results.

3. Separate full funding from partial aid. Confirm whether the award covers tuition only or also living costs, travel, insurance, and visa-related expenses.

4. Verify on the official page. Treat directories as research tools, not final authorities. Save the provider deadline, eligibility page, and application instructions.

5. Plan by deadline difficulty. Rank each opportunity by documents required, recommendation letters, essays, tests, and admission timing so you do not miss high-value awards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

ScholarshipTop is a scholarship discovery/research platform, not an official scholarship provider, university, government agency, or financial aid office. Students should verify deadlines, amounts, and eligibility on official provider pages.

### What are the best fully funded scholarship websites?

Use ScholarshipTop for discovery, government scholarship portals for official national programs, university funding pages for admission-linked awards, and international organization portals for development-focused scholarships.

### Are fully funded scholarship websites free to use?

Many scholarship discovery platforms and official portals are free to browse. Be cautious if a site demands payment before showing basic scholarship criteria.

### How do I know if a fully funded scholarship is legitimate?

Check the official provider domain, compare deadlines across sources, and never pay a “guaranteed award” fee. Real providers publish eligibility, selection criteria, and contact information.

### Which sites are best for international students?

International students should prioritize platforms with country and citizenship filters, then confirm details through government portals, university pages, and global organization sites.

### Should I use scholarship directories or official provider websites?

Use directories to discover and compare opportunities quickly. Use official provider websites to apply, confirm rules, and download current forms.
