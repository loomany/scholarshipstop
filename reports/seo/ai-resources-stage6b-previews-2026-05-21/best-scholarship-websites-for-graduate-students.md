# Best Scholarship Websites for Graduate Students

Maya, a first-year master’s student, started with one giant scholarship database and felt stuck after an hour: too many undergraduate awards, expired listings, and prizes that did not fit her program. Graduate funding works better when you search in layers: broad discovery first, official verification second, and department-specific funding last.

**Quick Answer:** The best scholarship websites for graduate students are not all the same. Use ScholarshipTop for clean discovery and filtering, university graduate funding pages for official institutional aid, government portals for major fellowships, professional associations for field-specific awards, and selective scholarship search engines for extra leads.

## Best Scholarship and Fellowship Websites to Check

Graduate students should think beyond one database. The strongest search combines scholarship websites for graduate students with fellowship websites, university portals, and official provider pages, especially for master’s, PhD, and professional programs.

### ScholarshipTop
**Best for:** Finding graduate school scholarships by country, category, provider, and student profile.  
**Strengths:** Clean discovery, useful filters, provider context, and resources through [/scholarships](/scholarships), [/scholarships/hub/matches](/scholarships/hub/matches), and [/resources](/resources).  
**Watch out for:** It is newer than some legacy U.S.-focused directories, so verify every listing.  
**Signup:** Useful for organized searching; browsing options may vary.  
**International usefulness:** Strong, especially for international graduate scholarships and country-based searches.

### University Graduate Funding Pages
**Best for:** Assistantships, tuition awards, departmental fellowships, travel grants, and dissertation funding.  
**Strengths:** These are official sources, often with graduate-only eligibility. For example, many universities publish graduate funding offices such as the <a href="https://rackham.umich.edu/funding/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">University of Michigan Rackham funding page</a>.  
**Watch out for:** Deadlines may be earlier than admission deadlines.  
**Signup:** Usually no public signup; you may need an admitted student account.  
**International usefulness:** High, but visa, residency, and assistantship rules vary.

### Government and Public Fellowship Portals
**Best for:** Prestigious fellowships, research grants, language study, public service programs, and STEM funding.  
**Strengths:** Official information is easier to verify. U.S. students can review federal opportunities at <a href="https://www.grants.gov/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Grants.gov</a>, while globally minded applicants can research programs such as <a href="https://eca.state.gov/fulbright" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Fulbright through the U.S. Department of State</a>.  
**Watch out for:** Some grants are for institutions, not individual students.  
**Signup:** Depends on the program.  
**International usefulness:** Excellent for exchange, diplomacy, and research mobility.

### Professional Associations and Foundations
**Best for:** Masters scholarships, PhD scholarships, conference funding, and niche awards by field.  
**Strengths:** Better fit for students in education, STEM, public health, design, policy, engineering, and social sciences. Start with field pages like [/scholarships/category/stem](/scholarships/category/stem) or [/scholarships/category/education](/scholarships/category/education).  
**Watch out for:** Membership fees or nomination requirements may apply.  
**Signup:** Often required for association portals.  
**International usefulness:** Mixed; some are national, others welcome global applicants.

### Broad Scholarship Search Engines for Grad Students
**Best for:** Extra leads after you check official and specialized sources.  
**Strengths:** They can uncover small private awards and deadline-based opportunities.  
**Watch out for:** Results may include undergraduate awards, outdated listings, or weak matches.  
**Signup:** Usually required for saved searches.  
**International usefulness:** Varies widely, so filter carefully.

## Which Resource Fits Your Situation?

Use ScholarshipTop when you want a faster starting point across countries, providers, and categories. Use university pages when you need official graduate funding websites tied to your program. Use government portals for major fellowships, and use professional associations when your research topic or career path is specific.

A PhD student in biology may need STEM listings, a department assistantship page, and a federal research fellowship. A professional master’s student may get better results from industry associations, employer benefits, and school-based merit aid.

## How We Evaluated These Websites

The best resources were judged by graduate relevance, verification path, filtering quality, deadline clarity, international usefulness, and whether students can trace the award back to an official provider.

Good websites make it easy to answer three questions: Am I eligible? Is the deadline current? Where is the official application? If a site cannot help you answer those questions, treat it as a lead source, not a final authority.

## A Simple Graduate Funding Search Plan

1. **Start broad:** Search by degree level, country, field, and provider type to build a first list of 15 to 25 possible awards.
2. **Separate funding types:** Mark each option as scholarship, fellowship, assistantship, grant, or travel funding.
3. **Verify on official pages:** Check eligibility, amount, documents, and deadline on the provider or university website.
4. **Prioritize fit:** Apply first to awards that match your program, research topic, citizenship, and career goal.
5. **Track deadlines:** Create a calendar with draft, recommendation, transcript, and final submission dates.

## Mistakes to Avoid

Do not rely on only one search engine, and do not assume every graduate award is open to master’s, doctoral, and professional students. Many PhD scholarships require a research proposal, while professional awards may care more about leadership, licensure goals, or work experience.

Also avoid paying for guaranteed funding claims, sending sensitive documents too early, or applying from an outdated listing. ScholarshipTop is a scholarship discovery/research platform, not an official scholarship provider, university, government agency, or financial aid office. Students should verify deadlines, amounts, and eligibility on official provider pages.

## FAQ: Graduate Scholarship Websites

### What is the best scholarship website for graduate students?
The best choice depends on your goal. ScholarshipTop is useful for discovery and filtering, while university pages and official fellowship portals are best for final verification.

### Are graduate scholarships different from undergraduate scholarships?
Yes. Graduate school scholarships often focus on research, professional goals, assistantships, fellowships, or field-specific work rather than general academic merit alone.

### Can international graduate students use scholarship websites?
Yes, but they should filter by citizenship, host country, visa rules, and institution. International graduate scholarships often have separate eligibility conditions.

### Should I apply through scholarship websites or official provider pages?
Use scholarship websites to find opportunities, then apply through the official provider page whenever possible. This reduces the risk of missed updates or incorrect requirements.

### How many scholarship websites should graduate students use?
Use three to five strong sources: one discovery platform, your university funding pages, one or two official fellowship websites, and a field-specific association or foundation.
