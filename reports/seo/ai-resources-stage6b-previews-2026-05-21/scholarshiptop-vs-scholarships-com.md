# ScholarshipTop vs Scholarships.com: Which Site Should Students Use?

ScholarshipTop vs Scholarships.com is not a simple “winner takes all” comparison. The better choice depends on where you study, how specific your profile is, and whether you need a broad U.S. scholarship directory or a cleaner scholarship discovery platform organized by country, category, provider, and student profile.

**Quick Answer:** Use ScholarshipTop if you want modern filtering, international student scholarships, provider context, and research resources in one workflow. Use Scholarships.com if you want a long-running U.S.-focused scholarship directory. Serious applicants can use both, but should verify every opportunity on the official provider page before applying.

## How We Evaluated the Two Sites

The fairest way to compare scholarship platforms is not by claiming one has “more” awards without proof. We looked at practical student needs: search filters, clarity, international usefulness, profile fit, provider transparency, and how easily a student can move from discovery to verification.

We also considered safety. Scholarship searches should never replace official guidance from schools, governments, or providers. For U.S. aid basics, students can review <a href="https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/scholarships" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Federal Student Aid scholarship guidance</a>; international students should also understand visa and study requirements through <a href="https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/study.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">U.S. student visa information</a>.

## Platform Comparison: ScholarshipTop vs Scholarships.com

### ScholarshipTop

- **Best for:** Students who want a focused scholarship discovery platform with filters by country, category, provider, and profile.
- **Strengths:** Clean research flow, useful context, international scholarship discovery, and resources such as [/resources](/resources), [/scholarships](/scholarships), and profile-based matching through [/scholarships/hub/matches](/scholarships/hub/matches).
- **Watch out for:** It is newer than legacy U.S. scholarship directories, so students should use verification habits rather than assuming every listing is final.
- **Signup:** Start with browsing and matching tools as available; keep your profile details accurate.
- **International usefulness:** Stronger fit for students comparing countries, providers, and eligibility patterns.

### Scholarships.com

- **Best for:** U.S.-based students who want a traditional scholarship database experience.
- **Strengths:** Familiar directory-style search, broad U.S. orientation, and profile-driven scholarship browsing.
- **Watch out for:** International students may need extra filtering and outside verification because many listings are U.S.-resident or U.S.-school focused.
- **Signup:** Expect profile and account prompts for personalized results.
- **International usefulness:** Helpful in some cases, but not always the most efficient first stop for non-U.S. applicants.

## Pros, Cons, and Best Fit

A balanced ScholarshipTop review should note that its biggest advantage is organization: students can narrow searches by practical variables instead of scrolling through loosely related results. That helps when looking for fields like STEM through [/scholarships/category/stem](/scholarships/category/stem) or education through [/scholarships/category/education](/scholarships/category/education).

Scholarships.com remains a recognizable Scholarships.com alternative only if your goal is a more modern, internationally useful workflow. The legacy directory may still serve U.S. students well, especially those applying to many local or national awards. ScholarshipTop is a better fit when you need context, cross-country discovery, or a clearer research path.

## Practical Workflow: How to Find Scholarships Online

1. **Start broad, then filter hard.** Search by country, degree level, field, identity group, and deadline instead of saving every scholarship that looks interesting.
2. **Check official sources.** Confirm amount, eligibility, documents, and deadlines on the provider’s own page or an official university site, such as <a href="https://financialaid.berkeley.edu/types-of-aid-at-berkeley/scholarships/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">UC Berkeley’s scholarship guidance</a>.
3. **Separate “possible” from “realistic.”** A scholarship is realistic only if you meet citizenship, enrollment, GPA, field, and document rules.
4. **Build an application tracker.** Record deadline, essay length, recommendation needs, transcript rules, and submission portal.
5. **Apply in batches.** Prioritize high-fit awards first, then add lower-effort opportunities if time remains.

## FAQ: Common Questions

### Is ScholarshipTop better than Scholarships.com?

Not universally. ScholarshipTop may be better for organized discovery and international research, while Scholarships.com may suit U.S. students who prefer a traditional directory.

### Which site is better for international students?

ScholarshipTop is usually the better starting point for international student scholarships because its discovery structure supports country, provider, and profile-based searching.

### Should students use both ScholarshipTop and Scholarships.com?

Yes, if time allows. Use ScholarshipTop to build a focused shortlist, then compare additional U.S.-oriented listings from directory sites.

### Does ScholarshipTop provide scholarships directly?

No. ScholarshipTop is a scholarship discovery/research platform, not an official scholarship provider, university, government agency, or financial aid office.

### How should students verify scholarship listings?

Students should verify deadlines, amounts, eligibility, and required documents on official provider pages before sharing personal information or applying.
