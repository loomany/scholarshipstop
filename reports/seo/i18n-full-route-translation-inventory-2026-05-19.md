## Static pages parity gaps and UX decisions

### Fully translated ES/FR (53 pilot paths)

All routes in `STAGE2_PILOT_CANONICAL_PATHS` have published ES/FR copies in `lib/i18n/staticTranslations/` with:

- Title, meta description, H1, body, FAQ translated
- Self-canonical + hreflang (`en`, `es`, `fr`, `x-default`)
- Included in `locale-es-*` / `locale-fr-*` sitemaps when quality score ≥85
- Same production templates as English (`LocalizedProductionPage` → trust/legal/marketing/hub shells)

### `/fr/resources` filters — decision

**Stage 2: simplified hub is acceptable.** DB article grid and `ResourcesIndexToolbar` are gated by `locale === 'en'` in `ResourcesIndexPageContent.tsx` to prevent English CMS cards on French URLs.

**Stage 3A (optional):** Client-side search/filter on static guide cards only — no DB dependency.

**Stage 4B (required for full parity):** Translate top CMS articles, then re-enable grid + toolbar with localized bodies.

## Slug strategy for international SEO

- **Now:** `/es/{english-slug}`, `/fr/{english-slug}` — safest
- **DB pages:** Keep English slugs; locale prefix distinguishes
- **Later:** Localized slugs only for curated static pages with redirect map
- **Ranking:** Translated content + hreflang sufficient; slug localization is optional P3
